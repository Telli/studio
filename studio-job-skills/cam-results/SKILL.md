# Explain this job

One job, the same one as the "Explain this job" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `cam` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Walk me through the CAM job and tell me the cycle time, the tools, and anything to fix before cutting.

The CAM job is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: A walk through the job: its cycle time, its tools and what to fix before cutting.
