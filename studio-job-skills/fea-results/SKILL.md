# Explain these results

One job, the same one as the "Explain these results" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `fea` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Walk me through the results in the study and tell me what fails, by how much, and what to change.

The study is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: A walk through the results: what fails, by how much, and what to change.
