# Strength under load

One job, the same one as the "Strength under load" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `fea` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Check the part for strength under its working load. Ask me for the loads, supports and material you need, then tell me where it is weakest and what to change.

The part is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: A study with the stress plot, the peak stress, the safety factor and a pass or fail.
