# Print on a Bambu printer

One job, the same one as the "Print on a Bambu printer" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `print3d` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Slice the part and get it ready on my Bambu printer. Check with me before the print starts.

The part is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: The print opened in Bambu Connect or Bambu Studio, for you to check and start.
