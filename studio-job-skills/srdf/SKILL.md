# SRDF for MoveIt

One job, the same one as the "SRDF for MoveIt" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `robot-simulation` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Make a MoveIt SRDF for the assembly: its planning groups, end effectors and the collisions to ignore.

The assembly is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: A validated SRDF for MoveIt: planning groups, end effectors and collisions to ignore.
