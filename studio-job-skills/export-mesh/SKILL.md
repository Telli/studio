# STL and 3MF

One job, the same one as the "STL and 3MF" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `cad` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Export the part as STL and 3MF.

The part is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: An STL and a 3MF, saved beside the part.
