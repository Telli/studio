# Cut-ready DXF

One job, the same one as the "Cut-ready DXF" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `cad` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Make a cut-ready DXF of the part for laser or waterjet cutting.

The part is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: A DXF of the outline and holes to cut, each cut closed and drawn once.
