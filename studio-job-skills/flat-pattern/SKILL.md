# Flat pattern DXF

One job, the same one as the "Flat pattern DXF" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `cad` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Make the flat pattern of the part as a DXF with its bend lines, ready to cut.

The part is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: The flat pattern as a DXF, its bend lines on a layer of their own.
