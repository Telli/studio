# Check for SLS printing

One job, the same one as the "Check for SLS printing" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `cad` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Check the part for SLS printing: wall thickness, small holes and features, and powder trapped inside. Tell me what to change.

The part is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: Findings for SLS printing, each passing, to fix or to check, with the fix.
